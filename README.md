# NextRole

Career development tracker: goals, ACCA papers, wins, learning hours, next-role planning and a resume builder. Installable web app for iPhone, iPad, Android and PC.

## What it tracks

- **Home:** next role, ACCA progress, coming up (exams, classes, interviews), study plan status and nudges.
- **Goals:** goals with progress and next actions.
- **ACCA:** papers and exemptions (current and Sept 2027 syllabus), attempts and result slips, a study plan with weekly hours per booked exam, classes and attendance, and a PER tracker (36 months of experience, 9 performance objectives, supervisor sign-off).
- **Career:** a job hunt board (Saved → Applied → Interview → Offer) with links to LinkedIn, JobStreet, Indeed and your own sites, interview prep with STAR answers and practice, a salary tracker with raise % and the pay range for your next role, and a vault for certificates and other files.
- **Journal:** wins, learning hours, monthly reviews, and an appraisal pack (print, Word or text).
- **Profile:** resume builder (ATS-friendly, print or Word), ATS check, jobs, education and skills.

Data stays on the device. Optionally it syncs to your own Google Sheet, Calendar and Drive through `Code.gs` (Google Apps Script, version 4). Exam result slips go to the "NextRole Results" Drive folder; other files go to "NextRole Vault".
